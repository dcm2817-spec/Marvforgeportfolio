About

A little about how I think.

I'm Marvellous, a student, a self-directed developer, and a writer. I'm happiest when I'm learning something new and can't yet see where it will lead.

Technology interests me because it's a way of solving real problems, and I teach myself by building things and working out what went wrong. Writing is the other half of how I think: it makes me slow down, say what I actually mean, and share it clearly.

Most of what I do comes from one habit: taking an idea that only exists in my head and turning it into something other people can use, read, or feel.
